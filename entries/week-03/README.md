## Reflection

Last week, the team and I met with Pippin. He gave specifics on what he was looking for and clarified his brief. It was nice to have been able to speak to him openly and get his perspective, especially with the writing of his book. This week I spent time on the learning contract, outlining my objectives and learning activities and working on how to best help my group in this project. 

Before the meeting, our sketches were based on levels of coding (sliders, node-based). Pippin's answers to our questions made it clear that the tool should be made for people with no coding knowledge, to more freely iterate on game design choices. Having this moment during the interview felt like we got much better direction for our project. 

I will be focusing on iterating prototypes in Figma instead of coding. I want to get comfortable with interactive components, variables and conditional logic within Figma. I was able to set objectives in the contract around building a prototype, testing it with non-coders, and iterating after each revision.
